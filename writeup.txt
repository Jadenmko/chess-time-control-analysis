Does time control widen or narrow the effect of rating gap on win percentage? Time control has almost no impact on how well rating gap predicts the winner in Blitz and Rapid, but it becomes a meaningful factor in Classical games. In Blitz and Rapid, the win-rate gap between huge and negligible rating differences is 28.10% and 28.15% — nearly identical. In Classical, that gap is 20.87%, about 7.3 points lower. This shows skill gap matters significantly in Blitz and Rapid, and somewhat less in Classical. Sample size matters here too: Blitz, Rapid, and Classical each had hundreds to thousands of games, while Bullet had only 131 across all skill gaps, making its results unreliable — so Bullet was excluded. This research should inform skill-based matchmaking on online chess platforms: Blitz and Rapid should use tighter rating-range matchmaking, while Classical can use looser ranges, to keep games fair across all time controls.
